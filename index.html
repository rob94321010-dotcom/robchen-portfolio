女人島
Women's Island
阿洛·卡力亭·巴奇辣 Ado Kaliting Pacidal · 2021
Full ZH→EN translation for indigenous Taiwanese artist Ado Kaliting Pacidal's album Women's Island: 7 song lyrics, album concept essay, and 3 international Austronesian musician bios (Malaysia, New Zealand, Indonesia). Content spans Pangcah indigenous culture, Austronesian identity, cross-island feminism, and environmental advocacy.
負責原住民音樂人阿洛·卡力亭·巴奇辣《女人島》中歌詞的中文英譯：包括7首歌詞、專輯概念文案，以及3位國際南島音樂人簡介（馬來西亞、紐西蘭、印尼）。內容涵蓋巴賽族原住民文化、南島語族認同、跨島嶼女性主義與生態議題。
