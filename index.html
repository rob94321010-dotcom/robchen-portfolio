〈一位父親納稅者的獨白〉
The Monologue of a Taxpayer as a Father
National Taxation Bureau · 2022 · Grand Prize (金賞)
財政部國稅局 · 2022年 · 首獎（金賞）
Written for the National Taxation Bureau's "Poet Write for You" bilingual poetry competition. Takes the perspective of a taxpaying father reflecting on civic duty and parenthood. Won the Grand Prize (金賞 / 1st Prize) from open nationwide submissions. Both Chinese and English versions authored by Rob; produced as an official bilingual video by the organizer.
受財政部國稅局「文青稅客，為你寫詩」雙語詩徵文活動之邀而作，以父親納稅者視角寫下對納稅義務與親職身份的內心獨白。獲選首獎（金賞），由主辦單位製作為官方雙語影片，中英文均由作者本人撰寫。

A bilingual poetry collection published by Showwe Information. Upon publication, the author was interviewed by The Roundtable: A Journal of Poetry (Hong Kong, vol.66) and invited to share work on Hansheng Radio's "Learning is Now" programme. Since publication, the author's prose and poetry have been regularly featured in major Taiwan media including United Daily News (聯合報) and The Liberty Times (自由時報), as well as literary journals including Chien Kun Poetry Quarterly《乾坤詩刊》, Lotus Literature, and People Fish Magazine — with the most recent publication in April 2026.
